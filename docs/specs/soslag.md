# Søslag i Svanemøllebugten (issue #11)

A playable level: two H-boats duel in the bay with water guns. Each crew tries to get the other crew as wet as possible
while still obeying the right-of-way rules, and the wind decides who has the advantage, because a water jet is carried
by the wind (long range downwind, almost nothing upwind). It is a real KØS summer exercise, so the tone is "fun and
friendly", never "war".

Status: SPEC + PLAN, revised after review (nothing built yet; see "Review notes" at the end). Branch `soslag`, based on `main`. Part 1 is the spec, Part 2 the build plan.
Language: Danish is the default UI language, English second. Trainers are always "Træner <name>" (the word
"Coach" never appears in new UI text; it only occurs as the repo helper names `coachFor` / `coachName` in code references). Wind is stored in knots and shown only through `KOS.U.windTxt` / `windVal` (m/s by default).

# Part 1 - SPEC

## 1. Goals and non-goals

Goals
- One new level, **Søslag**, in the bay: 1 player H-boat vs 1 AI H-boat, 3 minutes, water guns, wet meters.
- Teach tactics through play: get to windward, keep the distance right, tack at the right moment, give way when you
  must, refill the tank. A foul costs a 360 degree penalty turn (the race rules are reused, not rewritten).
- Phone first: one extra button (SKYD) is the whole gun interface. The boat is steered and sheeted exactly like in a race.
- Reuse: `KOS.Physics`, `KOS.Wind`, `KOS.Rules.monitor`, `KOS.AI.createHelm`, `KOS.SailScene` (overlay), `KOS.Effects`,
  `KOS.Input.attach` extra buttons, the results/badge pipeline. No new engine.
- Deterministic sim (fixed `KOS.DT` step, seeded `U.rng`) and a unit-testable pure core (`js/core/soslag.js`).

Non-goals (v1)
- No 2-player / network, no 2 vs 2, no dinghy fleet, no RIB target (see "Later").
- No free manual aiming (compass-style aim), no weapon variety, no damage other than the wet meter.
- No change to the H-boat physics, to `race.js`, or to the hub (no new hub area).
- Nothing about "hurting": crews only get wet. The UI says "våd" (wet), never "ramt" or "dræbt".

## 3. Round flow

1. **Intro card** (`.school-card.race-intro-card` with a `.sc-go` button, as in race, so the smoke test dismisses it).
   Træner Nicolas explains in 4 short lines: the wind carries the jet; sail to windward; give way; and the way back in for the boat
   to leeward ("tack away, or close in to under 7 m where you can reach him", section 5). Shows the wind
   (`windTxt`) and the controls (SKYD button / key K). The sim does not run until the card is closed.
2. **Countdown** 3-2-1, horn at "go". Boats start 45 m apart on a line across the wind, both on a beam reach at
   2.5 m/s, guns locked until the horn. Tanks start full.
3. **Duel**: `durationS = 180` s of sim time. The HUD timer counts down (`hud.update({timer: ms})`). With 15 s left Nicolas
   says the line `soslag.tip.last` (one key; no literal text elsewhere).
4. **End** when any of: time up (horn), a crew reaches **100 % wet** (soaked, round ends at once), or the player quits.
   Order inside one sim step: move, jets and hits, wet clamp to 100, the 100 % check, then the clock check. Both crews reaching 100 in
   the same step is a draw; if the clock ends on the same step in which one crew reaches 100, the 100 % rule decides (it is checked
   first). Otherwise the wetter crew loses. A short "Slut!" card, then `host.finish(result)` after a 1.2 s delay (as race does).
5. **Results screen** (standard) plus an injected wet-meter comparison (two bars) before `.results-stats`, using the
   same `injectTable` pattern as `race.js:240-263` (`KOS.Events.on('screen', fn)`, reads `KOS.App.run.lastResult`), keyed on
   `activity.mode === 'soslag'` and on a new result field `result.soslag = {myWet, oppWet, myHits, oppHits}` (race passes `result.raceTable`
   the same way). `app.js` stores the whole result object in `run.lastResult`, so no `app.js` change is needed; Step 3 checks it.
   The result is also built when the round is ended early (`debug.end()`, section 14).

Arena: a circle of radius `arenaR = 110` m around `O`, drawn as a dashed ring in the overlay. `O` is found by probing
`venue.courseArea` (fallback `{-290,-640,r:260}`) with the `okSpot` test (no land, depth >= draft + 0.6, not in the swim
zone, 8-point margin ring) at radius 110; if no spot passes, retry at 90, then 75. The ring is soft: outside it the gun
is locked and Nicolas says "Tilbage til banen!". The AI never targets a point outside 0.85 x `arenaR`.

## 4. Controls

Steering and sheeting are identical to race (the app, not the mode, owns the pause button, `app.js:636`): `KOS.Input.attach(host.layer, {layout:'sail', spinnaker:false, hike,
autoTrim, pauseButton:false, extraButtons})` plus `KOS.SailAids` for the jib. The spinnaker is off in this level (a
tactical duel, and one button less).

Extra buttons (in this order, so digits 1 and 2 also work):
1. `fire` - icon `drop`, label `input.fire` ("SKYD" / "SQUIRT"; the string goes in the `STR` table of `js/ui/input.js`, da and en, which
   `tools/test-input.js` reads by key), key `K`. **Hold to fire** (`ctrl.state.buttons.fire` is true while held, on touch and keyboard).
   Key check: `KEYMAP` in `input.js:508` has no `K` and no `T`; extra buttons match `e.key` case-insensitively and digits `1`/`2` map to the
   extra buttons by index, so `K`, `T`, `1`, `2` do not clash with the sail layout (`tools/test-input.js` is re-run in step 3).
2. `turn` - as in race (`T`); it pulses (`ctrl.highlight('turn', true)`) only while the player owes a turn and steers it himself, see the single rule in section 8.

Touch layout of SKYD (all sizes): at least 44x44 px, placed by the sheet area, never overlapping the sheet lever, the steering control or the
`turn` button. Checked at 844x390, 390x844 **and 320x568** (new `VIEWPORTS` entry `{ w: 320, h: 568, touch: true }` in `tools/qa-layout.js`;
`--vp` only filters that list). Multi-touch: `press()` (`input.js:260`) captures each pointer, so a second finger holds SKYD while the first
steers. Sliding the finger off the button does **not** release it (pointer capture, same as the race buttons); `pointerup`, `pointercancel`
and `lostpointercapture` all release it, and the key path releases on `keyup` and on window `blur` (`input.js:553`). Release always stops
fire: the flag is read from `ctrl.state.buttons.fire` each step and never latched.

**Aiming is automatic.** While SKYD is held the crew aims at the opponent's crew disc (section 5) and
`KOS.Soslag.solveAim` picks azimuth and elevation so the jet lands on the opponent, leading a moving target and allowing
for wind drift. The player controls *position* (wind, distance, timing) and *when to spend water*. A random spread
keeps it from being a laser: azimuth sigma 5 degrees (assist easy 3.5, pro 6.5) **and** elevation sigma 3 degrees, on top of the error from
the target's own turning (the constant-velocity lead). At 12 m the azimuth term moves the impact about 1.0 m and the elevation term about
0.8 m, so roughly 55-65 % of aimed jets hit the 1.4 m crew disc while the boats are steady, and fewer in a tack. If the opponent is out of reach the jet falls
short: the aim line turns orange and a "For langt!" hint shows. This is the simplest fun option on a phone (one thumb), and
every hit is still earned by sailing well.

Feedback while holding: a dotted aim line from the gun to the predicted landing point (green = lands on the crew,
orange = short, grey = gun locked), a rate-limited `spray` sound (limited on **sim time**, a counter advanced in `simStep`, never `performance.now()`) and a small muzzle puff.
Keyboard: `K` (hold) or `1` to fire; `T` or `2` penalty turn; everything else as race. The gun is **locked** (button greys
out) while: not started, a penalty turn is owed (+1.5 s after), outside the arena, tank empty, or the round is over.
**Rate**: one jet per `1/RATE` s (0.12 s) through a time accumulator that carries the remainder (`acc += dt; while (acc >= 0.12 && canFire) { launch(); acc -= 0.12; }`;
`acc` resets to 0 on release or lock). 0.12 s is 7.2 steps of `KOS.DT = 1/60`, so a step count would drift; the accumulator gives 8.33/s on average.

## 5. Water-jet physics (pure, in `js/core/soslag.js`)

Units: metres, seconds, m/s. A jet ("packet") has ground position `(x, y)`, height `z`, ground velocity `(vx, vy)` and
vertical speed `vz`. It is 2.5D: the ground track decides hits against the crew, `z` decides height and drawing.

Constants (`KOS.Soslag.CFG`, tunable in step 3):

| Name | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `V0` | 16 m/s | muzzle speed relative to the shooter's boat |
| `Z0` | 1.2 m | muzzle height above water |
| `G` | 9.8 m/s^2 | gravity on `vz` |
| `K` | 1.0 1/s | air drag: horizontal velocity relaxes towards the air velocity |
| `KW` | 1.3 | gameplay exaggeration of the wind speed felt by the jet |
| `EL_MIN`, `EL_MAX` | 8, 40 deg | elevation range `solveAim` may pick |
| `CREW_R` | 1.4 m | radius of the crew cylinder |
| `CREW_Z` | 0.3 - 2.3 m | jet height band that counts as "on a person" (deck 0.6 m + a seated crew) |
| `AIM_Z` | 1.2 m | height at which `solveAim` makes the jet cross the crew centre (chest height), **not** z = 0 |
| `RATE` | 1/0.12 = 8.33 packets/s | accumulator, section 4 |
| `MAX_JETS` | 64 | live packets at most (pooled, preallocated). At 8.33/s and a 2.2 s flight a boat has about 19 live jets, two boats about 38; the 3 s age cap gives a worst case of about 50 for two boats, so 64 is safe. **Overflow policy** if the pool is ever full: recycle the oldest jet (never skip the new one, never allocate). |

Per sim step (`dt = KOS.DT`), for each jet:
```
w   = wind.at(j.x, j.y);  air = U.vec(w.dir + PI) * U.ms(w.speed) * KW   // wind blows TOWARDS dir+PI
j.vx += (air.x - j.vx) * K * dt;  j.vy += (air.y - j.vy) * K * dt         // drag towards the air
j.vz -= G * dt;  j.x += j.vx*dt;  j.y += j.vy*dt;  j.z += j.vz*dt
```
Launch: ground velocity = `V0 * cos(el) * U.vec(az)` + the boat's velocity (`boat.vx, boat.vy`); `vz = V0 * sin(el)`; the
start point is the gun position (boat centre + `U.vec(heading) * 0.15 * L`) at height `Z0`. The jet ends when `z <= 0`
(splash) or its age exceeds 3 s.

Reference ranges (a scratch simulation of exactly these constants, still boat, `el` chosen for maximum range, +/- 0.5 m; the step 2 test re-checks them. The first
draft's `V0 15, K 1.2, KW 1.7` gave only 5.1 m upwind at 9 kn and was retuned):

| True wind | shooting downwind (el 40) | 0 kn | shooting upwind (best el) | sideways drift of a 20 degree crosswind shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 kn (3.1 m/s) | ~16 m | ~11 m | ~8.5 m (el 19) | ~2.3 m |
| 9 kn (4.6 m/s) | ~18.7 m | ~11 m | ~7.5 m (el 15.5) | ~3.5 m |
| 12 kn (6.2 m/s) | ~21.3 m | ~11 m | ~6.7 m (el 13) | ~4.6 m |

The windward shooter reaches 2.5 times (9 kn) to 3.2 times (12 kn) as far as the leeward one: a real advantage, but the leeward boat is **not
disarmed**: hulls are 8.3 m, so at overlap distance (under about 7 m) both can hit. The way back in is taught in the intro and by the
`soslag.tip.windward` tip: tack away (the AI follows to a new station) or close in under 7 m. The AI holds its station at 0.7 x downwind range (about
13 m at 9 kn), so a leeward player can always sail at him until he is inside his own upwind range. Tests assert `downwind / upwind >= 2.4` and
`upwind >= 7.0 m` at 9 kn.

**Hit test** (swept, per step, per jet, against the opponent's *current* state): the segment from the previous to the new
jet position against the **crew cylinder** (disc centre `boat + U.vec(heading + PI) * 0.12 * L`, the cockpit; radius `CREW_R`) with `U.segDistance` (2D);
`z` interpolated at the closest point must be inside `CREW_Z`. It counts when both hold. Otherwise:
- the jet enters the hull capsule (`Physics.capsule(boat)`) outside the crew disc, or is above/below the band: **hull/sail
  hit, 0 points**, a small splash and a dull sound;
- the jet reaches `z <= 0`: water splash, 0 points.
A jet only wets the *other* crew (owner check). Jets ignore land.

`solveAim(shooter, target, wind, opts)` (pure) returns `{az, el, ok, miss, T, zHit, descent}`. Iterate 3 times: predict the target crew
position at the previous flight time `T` (constant velocity), bisect `el` in `[EL_MIN, EL_MAX]` so the jet is at **height `AIM_Z`** (on its way
down) over the crew centre, then correct `az` by the lateral miss. `ok` is true when a final forward simulation of the aimed jet is **inside the
cylinder** (horizontal miss <= `CREW_R` **and** `z` inside `CREW_Z`) at some step; it is not a landing-distance test. Out of reach: `ok:false`, `el`
at the best elevation, the jet falls short.
**Descent angle (measured with the scratch simulation, 9 kn):** a direct 10 m downwind shot comes down at about 27 degrees, a 10 m crosswind shot at 44,
a 7 m upwind shot at 54, a maximum-range downwind lob at 52-66. A horizontal error `e` changes the height at the crew by about `e * tan(descent)`, so with
`AIM_Z 1.2` and the band 0.3-2.3 the effective horizontal tolerance is about +/-2 m at 27 degrees but only about +/-0.7 m at 54 degrees: steep (upwind,
long) shots are harder, which is intended. Unit tests assert that every `ok` result is a hit when sigma is 0 (never "too high" or "too low"), and that a
0.5 m aim offset still hits at 27 and at 54 degrees. The caller adds `opts.sigma` (radians, azimuth and elevation, section 4) with the seeded rng so tests
stay deterministic. The "Præcision" stat is hits / jets fired; with these sigmas the autopilot at normal assist must land at 40-70 % (a stat above 80 %
fails the balance test, because then the stat means nothing).

## 6. Tank and refill

- Capacity 100 units; each packet costs 1.5 (66 packets = 8 s of continuous fire).
- Passive refill 2.0 units/s ("pumpen fylder lidt hele tiden"): 360 units over 180 s, so passive supply alone sustains about 36 % firing time and the tank,
  not the clock, is the real limit; from empty it takes 50 s, a slow fallback that makes dipping worth it (3.0/s rejected, it would make dipping pointless).
- **Dipping**: when the boat's speed is below 1.2 m/s (about 2.3 kn; an H-boat luffed head to wind gets there in 3-5 s, and the easy-assist helm can reach it)
  and it is not in a penalty, refill is 14 units/s ("Dyp spanden!"). The HUD tank gauge shows a bucket icon, and the hint `soslag.hud.dip` shows when
  tank < 25 and speed >= 1.2 (at most every 20 s). Refilling is a tactical cost (a slow boat is an easy target)
  without a refill button, which suits a phone.
- Empty tank: gun click; the button stays grey until the tank has 8 units (hysteresis, no flicker).
- Per-boat plain fields on the physics boat objects (like `b.rc` in race): `b.tank`, `b.wet` (0..100), `b.gunLock` (s), `b.pen`.

## 7. Wet meter, winning and stars

- Each packet that hits the crew cylinder adds `WET_GAIN = 0.48` points (0.35 in the first draft; tuned in step 4 so the autopilot at normal lands 55-75 % opponent wet) to that crew's wet meter (0-100, clamped). No drying in a 3 minute game.
  Arithmetic: a full tank is 66 jets, at 55-60 % hits about 36-40 hits = 13-14 points. A good game spends about 100 + 360 (passive) + two dips
  (about 120) = 580 units = 390 jets = about 215 hits = **70-75 points**, so about 70 % is reachable for a good player who holds the windward position,
  a knock-out to 100 needs a near-perfect duel and stays rare, and a typical result is 35-60 %. `WET_GAIN` is the one balance constant; step 5 tunes it
  so the autopilot at normal assist averages 55-75 % opponent wet.
- **Win**: the lower wet meter at the end. Reaching 100 % ends the round at once and that crew loses ("Gennemblødt!").
  A difference under **5 points** is a draw ("Uafgjort - flot kamp!", counted like a loss for stars: 1 star). Both crews at 100 in the same step is also a draw (section 3).
- **Stars** (`result.stars`; `success = stars > 0`, which is exactly the `evalBadges` rule `ok = success !== false && stars > 0`, `app.js:115`):
  - 0: the player fired fewer than 20 jets (idle, or never joined in: try again); `success = false`
  - 1: lost or drew, but fought (fired >= 20 jets)
  - 2: won
  - 3: won by >= 15 points **and** at most 1 foul (fair play). Balance target: 3 stars on at most 30 % of 20 seeds for the autopilot at normal.
- `score = max(0, round(oppWet*10 + max(0, 100 - myWet)*3 - fouls*50))`.
- `result.stats`: `'soslag.stat.wetOpp'`, `'soslag.stat.wetMe'`, `'soslag.stat.hits'`, `'soslag.stat.acc'` (accuracy %),
  `'soslag.stat.fouls'` (five separate keys in both languages, section 12). `msgKey` is one of `soslag.msg.win|lose|draw|soaked|soakedMe`; `titleKey` from the standard
  `ui.results.*` set; `timeMs` is the sim time at the end.
- **Badge** `soslag` (da "Søslagsmester", en "Water-fight champion"): a `BADGES` entry in `js/ui/app.js` plus
  `give('soslag', a.mode === 'soslag' && ok && (result.stars || 0) >= 2)` in `evalBadges`; `tools/check-badges.js` must pass.
  Other badges (`app.js:112-126`): `first-sail` and `three-stars` are granted automatically; `first-race-win`, `podium` and `race-five` sit inside
  `if (a.mode === 'race' && ok)`, so they do **not** fire here.

## 8. Right of way and penalties

Reuse the race infrastructure by copying and adapting `onFoul`, `penalize`, `addPen`, `penStep`, `penControls`
(`race.js:644-743`, about 100 lines); the per-boat penalty state lives in `b.pen` (not `b.rc`). It is not a pure copy; the foreign
dependencies and the mapping:
- Drop every `rc.finT` guard (nobody finishes here) and the whole mark branch (`f.type === 'mark'`, R31, committee): `monitor.update(live, wind, [], dt)` has no marks.
- `b.rc.pen` / `v.rc.pen` become `b.pen` / `v.pen` (plain field on the physics boat); `S.pen` still holds the player's pen.
- `env.t` is the sim clock (`const now = env.t`), so the cooldowns (`S.cool`, `S.myFoulT`, `S.closeT`) run on sim time. Keep `S.cool = {}` in the state.
- `showCard`, `say`, `floatText`, `sfx`, `ctrl.highlight('turn', ...)` and the `host.layer` class (`race-pen` becomes `soslag-pen`) are closures in race.js, not shared:
  soslag.js defines its own.
- i18n: the copied code reads race's `race.pen.warn|easy|title360|do360|rightTitle|right|ai|done` strings and `rules.name.*` / `rules.reason.*`. That is a stated
  dependency on race's keys (same page, loaded earlier; `check-i18n.js` covers them); soslag adds its own keys only for new text.
- **Collision escalation owner**: `simStep` calls `Physics.collide(live, venue, [])` (as `race.js:832`), which returns `[{type:'boat', a, b, speed}]`; `speed` is the
  closing speed along the contact normal (`physics.js:576`). `simStep` stores `S.lastHit[a.id + '|' + b.id] = {speed, t}`. `monitor.update` only emits fouls
  (offender, victim, rule, contact); `onFoul` looks the pair up in `lastHit` (within 0.5 s) and uses `turns = 2` when `speed >= 1.4`, else 1. No foul, no escalation.
- `monitor = KOS.Rules.monitor({mode:'race', cooldown:1})`, `monitor.update(live, wind, [], dt)` (no marks, so R18/R31 are
  inactive). Fouls go through `onFoul` as in race: a boat in a penalty has no right of way (RRS 22.2), contact with a
  relative speed < 0.35 m/s is ignored, per-pair cooldown 12 s, player cooldown 15 s.
- **One penalty rule (all levels, both boats):** a contact foul by the give-way boat means that boat owes one 360 degree turn (`turns = 1`; 2 after a hard
  collision, below). The player and the AI follow the same rule at every assist level: the turn is the price, and the gun is locked while it is owed (+1.5 s after
  it is served). Assist changes only the help: on **easy** the player's turn is steered automatically (`pen.auto = true`, the helm turns the boat and avoids
  stalling) and the card is the friendly `warn` card; on normal and pro the player steers the turn and the `turn` button pulses until it is served. On easy the
  button does not pulse (the boat does it) and pressing it is a harmless no-op. The AI turns whenever it is the offender. (This replaces "easy: warning only", which
  let easy players ignore the rule while only the AI paid, and contradicted the pulsing button.) Card and title use `KOS.Rules.ruleName(rule)` and `t(f.reasonKey, f.reasonVars)`.
- **Collision** (boat-boat hit with closing `speed >= 1.4 m/s` plus a foul on the same pair, mapping above): the give-way boat takes **two** turns (720 degrees) and
  its gun is locked 3 s on top.
- **Gun locked while a turn is owed** (+1.5 s after it is served): the "you lose your shots" penalty; the other crew keeps
  shooting. Nothing else is lost (no water, no points), to keep it kind.
- Close calls without contact: Nicolas warns the player at most every 25 s with the rule reason (race behaviour). Rule names
  and reasons come from `rules.name.*` / `rules.reason.*`, tying into the rules school.
- The windward boat gives way (R11): the best shooting place is the place where you must keep clear. That tension is the
  point of the level. The AI respects it through `plan.mode = 'race'` (`ai.js keepClear`).

## 9. AI behaviour

One AI helm built as in race.js: `createBoat('hboat', ...)`, `b.helm = KOS.AI.createHelm(b, {skill, aggression, seed})`,
`b.pace = KOS.AI.paceFor(skill, fs)`. It follows a `{target}` plan recomputed at 4 Hz (every 15 steps). Never reuse one
plan object without changing x/y, and never let the target be reached: a reached target makes the helm "finish" and idle,
so the station is re-offset each update and `r = 0.5`. Penalty turns: `if (b.pen) c = penControls(b, c)`.

Difficulty: `fs = KOS.AI.fleetSkill(host.profile, host.assist, totalStars)`, `skill = clamp(lerp(fs.lo, fs.hi, 0.5) + 0.04)`;
`aimSigma = lerp(10, 5, skill)` degrees (azimuth; elevation sigma 3 as for the player); bursts of 0.8-1.6 s with 0.4-0.9 s pauses (shorter pauses at higher skill);
reaction delay 0.4 s down to 0.1 s. Assist easy also halves the AI's fire rate during the first 30 s.
**Kindness**: when the player is >= 85 % wet the AI halves its fire rate; it never fires at a boat outside the arena.

States (`b.ai.mode`):
1. **SEEK** - go to the windward station: `station = opp + U.vec(wd) * d + U.vec(wd + PI/2) * side * 6`, with
   `d = 0.7 * downwindRange(tws)` (about 12 m at 9 kn, clamped 9..14) and `side` chosen so the AI stays on the opponent's
   starboard side when possible. `navTo` does the tacking: a windward target is reached by beating, so "seek the windward
   position" needs no new helm code. The target is clamped to 0.85 x `arenaR`.
2. **SHOOT** - when `solveAim(...).ok`, tank >= 8 (25 after running empty), not in a penalty, not locked and not outside the
   arena: fire in bursts while still sailing to the station.
3. **CONTEST** - the opponent is windward within his downwind range: try to get back to windward by luffing/tacking (SEEK
   towards the opponent's windward point); the helm keeps clear by rule. If it gains nothing in 12 s, go to ESCAPE.
4. **ESCAPE** (dodge) - sail a close/beam reach away until the distance exceeds `0.9 * downwindRange` or the opponent leaves
   the windward sector (+/- 60 degrees); 10 s time-out so it never sulks.
5. **REFILL** - tank < 12: sail to a point `1.2 x downwindRange` crosswind of the opponent, luff to head-to-wind (speed
   < 1.2 m/s) and dip until tank >= 60, then SEEK. Abort when the opponent is within 12 m.

## 16. Risks and open points

- **Arena fits?** The bay is shallow and the swim zone is close; the `okSpot` probe at 110/90/75 m must find a spot for all
  seeds. Step 1 tests it with a sweep of 200 seeds.
- **Hit geometry feels unfair** from a top-down camera (height is invisible). Mitigation: the lift in `proj`, the aim line
  colour, the "too far" hint, the `AIM_Z` / cylinder hit test (section 5) and a generous `CREW_R`.
- **Fouls only on contact** (the race rule). At 10-13 m shooting distance fouls are rarer than close calls; accepted for v1
  (close calls are the trainer's lesson). A "forced to alter course" rule is a later improvement.
- **AI idling** when a target is reached (`helm.finished`): the station is re-offset every update and `r = 0.5`; tested.
- **Balance**: the constants are first guesses; step 3 tunes `V0/K/KW`, step 4 `aimSigma` and the fire rate with `autoplay`
  sims (3 stars should be rare, an idle player must lose).
- **Wind sign**: `dir` is where the wind comes FROM; the jet is carried towards `dir + PI`. Covered by unit tests.
- **Unlock** is decided: 30 stars (`SOSLAG_STARS`), a one-line constant if the club wants it different.
- **Club check**: the exercise rules (points, time, refilling, name) belong to the club; ask before release.
